## 💫 About Me:

### Hey there! I'm Vipul Kotkar, Analog Layout Engineer.

You can find me buried under a pile of DRC/LVS logs 📚, sipping coffee ☕, and occasionally questioning ❓ parasitic capacitances.

I work with cool folks on making advanced node chips 💻 that don't just hit frequency targets but also do a mean moonwalk in area efficiency. Think of it as my way of making silicon eco-friendly ♻️.

I'm that guy who finds joy in Analog Layout and thinks floorplanning is cooler than a front-end framework!! I'm basically a layout enthusiast, turning complex schematics into silicon magic spells.
